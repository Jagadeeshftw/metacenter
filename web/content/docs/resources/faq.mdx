### Is Metacenter affiliated with Stacks, Hiro or any protocol?

No. It is an independent open-source project (MIT). Forum quotes on the site are context, not endorsements.

### Why are some headline figures "mirrored"?

They come from the latest distribution's events. When `pox5-reader` is available on mainnet, the dashboard uses its onchain figures instead.

### Why does the testnet feed show mainnet numbers?

Testnet has almost no bond stake. The testnet `risk-feed` mirrors mainnet distributions, with every raw input stored so anyone can recompute them.

### Can the reserve cover a shortfall?

Not under the current contract. See [Reserve & hypothetical cover](/docs/metrics/reserve).

### Who is short-paid first?

The last bond in the payout order. See [Bond payout order](/docs/metrics/payout-order).

### How often is the data updated?

The indexer polls every ~10 minutes. Distributions happen once per 1,050 Bitcoin blocks.
