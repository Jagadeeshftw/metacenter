---
title: Introduction
description: Metacenter is an open risk feed for Stacks Bitcoin Staking (PoX-5).
---

Metacenter measures how safe the PoX-5 reward flow is, from public data only, and publishes the results three ways: a dashboard, a public JSON API, and Clarity contracts that other contracts can call.

<AsOf />

## What it answers

- **Can the reward pool pay what bonds are owed?** Coverage is <Metric name="coverage" />.
- **How far can the pool fall before bond yield is hit?** Headroom is <Metric name="headroom" />.
- **What backs a shortfall?** The reserve holds <Metric name="reserve" />, but the current contract cannot pay bonds from it.
- **What do STX-only stakers earn?** <Metric name="apy" />, annualised in BTC terms.
