**Now:** <Metric name="obligation" />

## Definition

`obligation = Σ over active bonds of shares × target-rate ÷ 10000 ÷ 50`, in sats per interval. This is exactly pox-5's per-bond target (<Pox5 lines="2266" />), summed.

- `shares` are the bond's staked sats for the cycle (`get-total-shares-staked-for-cycle`).
- A bond is active in a cycle when the cycle's calculation height falls inside its term.
