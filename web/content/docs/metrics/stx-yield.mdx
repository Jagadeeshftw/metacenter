**Latest:** <Metric name="yieldPerStx" />, or <Metric name="apy" /> a year in BTC terms.

## Definition

- **Per STX:** `total-stx-staker-rewards ÷ cycle-staked-ustx × 1,000,000`, in sats per STX for one distribution.
- **APY (BTC terms):** `per-STX yield × 50 ÷ STX/BTC price`, using the price at that distribution (an hourly price nearest to the `calculate-rewards` block).

Because the APY uses a price, it is <Tag kind="mirrored" />. The price source (CoinGecko, with a Coinbase fallback) and timestamp are stored with every distribution.
