---
title: Bond payout order
description: The order pox-5 pays bonds in, and who is short-paid first.
---

pox-5 pays bonds in **descending stx-value-ratio**, ties to the **lower bond index**, and each bond receives `min(target, what is left)`. The contract enforces the order (<Pox5 lines="2285-2299" />).
